DEEP SCAN MODE

Deep Scan: six passive sections, one run

The Deep Scan tool below goes further than the standard Domain Security Scan. It combines public, passive OSINT only: RDAP registration data, Certificate Transparency history, deeper DNS, an HTTP security-header report, expanded technology detection and a Wayback Machine archive check. No intrusive scanning or brute force. Results are grouped by section in the Deep Scan card below.

  • 1 · Registration (RDAP whois: registrar, dates, country)
  • 2 · Certificate Transparency (subdomains & cert history via crt.sh)
  • 3 · Deeper DNS (MX, NS, TXT, SPF/DMARC/DKIM, CAA, SOA, DNSSEC)
  • 4 · HTTP / security headers (HSTS, CSP and more, one polite GET)
  • 5 · Technology detection (headers, cookies and page source)
  • 6 · Archive history (Wayback Machine availability)

All sources are free and keyless. If a source is unreachable, that section reports its status and the rest of the scan still completes.
