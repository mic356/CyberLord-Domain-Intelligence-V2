OFFICIAL FOUNDER PROFILE

Ogbonna Michael Chinedu

Founder & Chief Executive Officer of CyberLord Domain Intelligence

About the founder

Ogbonna Michael Chinedu is the founder and creator of CyberLord Domain Intelligence. He leads the platform’s development and direction, with a focus on cybersecurity research, domain intelligence and practical digital security tools.

About CyberLord Domain Intelligence

CyberLord Domain Intelligence is an independently developed cybersecurity research and technology platform. Its tools are designed to help users examine publicly observable domain and web-security information, understand potential security issues and support responsible defensive research.

Platform capabilities include domain reconnaissance, DNS intelligence, website security analysis and cybersecurity learning resources. Security testing is intended for systems the user owns or is explicitly authorised to assess.
