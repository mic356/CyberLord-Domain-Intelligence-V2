ABOUT CYBERLORD

Your website should be safe. We help you make sure.

Who we are

CyberLord is a Nigerian cybersecurity service built in Lagos. We are not a big foreign company. We are a small team that saw how much money Nigerian small businesses lose to website problems they never saw coming, and we decided to build the tool we wished existed: a security check you can actually understand, with a clear explanation of what to fix first.

What CyberLord does

CyberLord checks websites for security problems and teaches you how to fix them. You enter a website address, we check it in under a minute, and you get a plain-language report that tells you what is wrong, how serious it is, and what to do about it. No technical background needed. If you want to learn more, the CyberLord Academy teaches you cybersecurity step by step, in simple words.

Why this matters to a small business owner

Your website is your shop. Customers trust it with their money, their email addresses, and their phone numbers. When a website has a security problem, criminals can steal customer data, use your site to scam your customers, or send your business emails to spam. Most business owners find out only after the damage is done. A CyberLord check takes less than a minute and shows you the problems while they are still yours to fix.

What we check

We look at the things criminals actually look at: your website's security settings, your email setup (so scammers cannot pretend to be you), whether your email address has leaked in data breaches, and whether anyone has registered a lookalike website to trick your customers. Each check returns evidence, not guesses. If a check cannot reach its source, we tell you to try again instead of showing you a fake result.

Who CyberLord is for

Small business owners who have a website but no IT person. Shop owners, restaurant owners, schools, churches, freelancers, and anyone who sells online in Nigeria. If you can type your website address into a box, you can use CyberLord.

What we will never do

We only check websites and addresses you own, or that you have permission to check. We do not attack anyone. We do not sell your data. We do not show you fake results to scare you into paying. Our free plan is free forever, with no card required, because we believe every business deserves to know if their website is safe.

Where to start

Start with the free check. It takes less than a minute and costs nothing. If you want deeper checks and the full academy, look at our plans.
